# Ad Brief

Fill in what you know. Leave the rest blank — the director will infer it and tell you what it
assumed, so you can correct cheaply before anything gets generated.

The three starred fields are the ones that actually change the output. Everything else is
useful but optional.

---

## The product

**What is it?** ★
<!-- Name, variant, what's in the box. Be concrete: "500ml argan oil shampoo,
     amber plastic bottle, matte white label" beats "hair product". -->


**What's it made of / what does it look like?**
<!-- Gloss, matte, metallic, transparent, fabric, edible, digital-only.
     This decides the lighting scheme, so it matters more than it sounds. -->


**Price point**
<!-- Rough band is fine. Drives register and pacing. -->


**Reference materials** ★
<!-- THE MOST IMPORTANT FIELD. Paths or links to real product photos, logo
     (transparent PNG if possible), brand colours, past ads.
     Without a real product photo the ad will show a product that isn't yours —
     the model invents the label. With one, image-to-video keeps it faithful. -->


---

## The buyer

**Who buys this?**
<!-- Age, situation, what they already use. A guess is fine; it'll be checked. -->


**What annoys them right now?**
<!-- The problem the product ends. -->


**What must they believe after watching?** ★
<!-- ONE sentence. This is the entire ad. If you list three things,
     you'll be asked to choose. -->


---

## The ad

**Where does it run?**
<!-- Instagram Reels / TikTok / Shorts / YouTube. Which accounts.
     Organic or paid. Cold audience or retargeting. -->


**Length**
<!-- Leave blank to get the platform-optimal length.
     15–30s suits Meta click-through; 9–15s suits TikTok. -->


**Tone you want**
<!-- Funny / straightforward / premium and atmospheric / like a real customer filmed it.
     Leave blank for a recommendation with reasoning. -->


**Anything that must appear**
<!-- Specific claim, price, offer, logo placement, legal line. -->


**Anything that must NOT appear**
<!-- Claims you can't legally make, competitor mentions, imagery to avoid. -->


**Ads you like** (links)
<!-- If the `watch` skill is installed, these get analysed properly —
     hook mechanism, structure, lighting, pacing. Very useful. -->
